- [Teaching and Pedagogical Resources (Society for Cinema and Media Studies - SCMS)](https://www.cmstudies.org)
- [Film Education and Research Framework (British Film Institute - BFI)](https://www.bfi.org.uk/education-research)

- [The Open Film School Master Guide & Roadmap (No Film School)](https://nofilmschool.com)
